Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

NIVAXA is based in Pune, India. We provide an AI phone receptionist, called Priya, to dental clinics. This page explains what information we handle when you call a clinic that uses it, and how our Google sign-in connection, Nivaxa Dental Clinic Calendar, uses Google Calendar data.

Information we collect

  • What a caller tells the assistant during a call: name, mobile number, preferred date and time, and the reason for the visit.
  • A record of the call, which may include a transcript, the time and length of the call, and the language used.
  • Appointment records: date, time, status, and any change or cancellation.
  • Delivery status of the WhatsApp messages we send you.

The assistant does not give medical advice and we do not ask callers for health records.

How we use it

  • To book, change and cancel appointments.
  • To send confirmations, reminders and follow-up messages on WhatsApp.
  • To alert clinic staff when a caller reports an urgent problem, and to connect the call to them.
  • To keep the clinic's schedule accurate and to count usage for billing.

Google Calendar data

With the clinic owner's permission, our connection reads, creates and deletes appointment events on the clinic's Google Calendar. We use this only to place and remove appointments booked by phone, and to check that a slot is free.

We do not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers listed below, and only to run the booking service. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The clinic owner can remove our access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.

Who processes the data for us

We use these providers to run the service. Each handles data only to deliver its part of it.

  • A voice AI platform that runs the phone calls (OmniDimension).
  • A database provider that stores appointment and call records (Supabase).
  • Our own workflow server, which moves information between the systems.
  • Google, for Google Calendar.
  • Meta, for WhatsApp messages.

We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as it is needed to run the service for the clinic, or as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Your choices

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, and you can withdraw your consent. You can also raise a complaint with us. Email meet2meghraj@gmail.com and tell us the mobile number you called from.

Security

We protect information with access controls and encrypted connections. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
