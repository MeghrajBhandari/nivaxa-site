NIVAXA

An AI phone receptionist for dental clinics in India.

NIVAXA is based in Pune. We build a voice assistant that answers a clinic's calls in English, Hindi and Marathi. It books, changes and cancels appointments, answers common questions about timings and services, and passes urgent calls to clinic staff.

Nivaxa Dental Clinic Calendar

This is the name of our Google sign-in connection. With the clinic owner's permission, it reads, creates and deletes appointment events on the clinic's own Google Calendar so that phone bookings appear there. It is used for nothing else. The details are in our privacy policy.

Messages to patients

After a booking, change or cancellation, patients receive a WhatsApp message with the details. Reminders and follow-up messages are sent the same way.
